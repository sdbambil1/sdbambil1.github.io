  {2026}

  development, adaptation, and temporal test cohorts.

  \item Evaluated updating and regularization across
  \textbf{22,500 simulated datasets} and five validation
  allocations, measuring performance on independent

  and regularization changed the relative performance
  of updating policies.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2pt}

\project
  {Fire Loss Modeling and Tail Diagnostics
   \textnormal{\small\enspace$\vert$\enspace R}}
  {2026}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Analyzed approximately
  \textbf{1.25 million positive-loss records} from
  NFIRS 2016--2025 using Peaks-Over-Threshold and
  Generalized Pareto models to estimate Value-at-Risk
  and Expected Shortfall.

  \item Compared empirical and fitted expectations within
  bounded loss layers; assessed uncertainty by
  incident-cluster bootstrap and examined sensitivity
  to exceptional records.
\end{itemize}
